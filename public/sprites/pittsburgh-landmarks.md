# Pittsburgh landmark atlas

`pittsburgh-landmarks.png` is a 3-column × 2-row transparent atlas generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Cells: Cathedral of Learning, hospital, police station, steel skyscraper, glass office tower, academic hall. The renderer gives landmarks different sizes while preserving their proportions.

The four navigation areas are a compressed game interpretation, not official administrative boundaries. Oakland's university and medical character follows https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/Business-Development/City-Planning/Master-Neighborhood-Plans/The-Oakland-Plan and https://www.pitt.edu/about/pittsburgh-campus . Downtown occupies the river confluence; the office corridor follows the Strip on the inside bank of the Allegheny. Simulation neighborhood IDs remain separate from navigation areas.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game asset: ONE transparent PNG sprite atlas, exactly 3 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells (overall 1536x1024), six separate Pittsburgh isometric buildings. Match detailed hand-painted pixel-style city builder art, 2:1 isometric diamond bases, lit upper left, crisp warm stone and brick and slate-blue windows. Each object fully contained in own cell with transparent margins; base bottom at 90 percent cell height. No background, no grid, no labels outside buildings. Top row left: recognizable Cathedral of Learning, very tall slender cream limestone Gothic skyscraper with stepped pinnacles and intricate vertical windows, broad low Gothic base, NOT a church spire. Top middle: UPMC-inspired substantial modern hospital complex, white limestone and blue glass midrise wings, rooftop helipad, ambulance entrance, blue medical cross. Top right: Pittsburgh police station, two-story red brick civic building, navy entrance canopy with small POLICE sign, parked blue-white patrol car. Bottom left: Pittsburgh downtown dark steel skyscraper inspired by US Steel Tower, tall triangular dark bronze black tower with many fine windows, compact plaza. Bottom middle: contemporary corporate headquarters, blue glass stepped office tower with landscaped stone plaza. Bottom right: University of Pittsburgh academic hall, grand cream stone neoclassical low building with columns and lawn. Cathedral and skyscraper tall in their cells, other buildings shorter. Consistent viewing angle. Actual transparent alpha outside each isolated diamond footprint. No trees surrounding buildings, only two small ornamental trees maximum per cell. Highly polished game sprites, no UI.

## Transparency refinement

Remove all background to true alpha transparency, preserving the six buildings and equal 3×2 cell layout. Keep transparent margins between cells, with no colored or checkerboard background.
